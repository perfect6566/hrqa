# Expense Reimbursement Policy

## Overview

This policy outlines the guidelines for submitting and receiving reimbursement for business-related expenses.

## General Guidelines

### Pre-Authorization
- Expenses under $50: No pre-approval required
- Expenses $50-$500: Manager approval required
- Expenses over $500: Director-level approval required
- Conference registration and travel: Submit request 2 weeks in advance

### Submission Timeline
- All expense reports must be submitted within 30 days of incurring the expense
- Expenses submitted after 60 days may not be reimbursed
- Submit through the expense portal with itemized receipts

## Eligible Expenses

### Travel

#### Airfare
- Economy class for flights under 6 hours
- Premium economy for flights 6+ hours (pre-approval for business class)
- Book through approved travel agency or use company booking tool
- Price must be reasonable (within 20% of lowest available)

#### Ground Transportation
- Taxi/Rideshare: Reasonable for business travel
- Rental Car: Compact class; upgrade with manager approval
- Personal Vehicle: $0.67 per mile (IRS standard rate)
- Parking: Reasonable expenses while on business travel
- Tolls: Reimbursed when traveling for business

#### Lodging
- Standard room rate at business-class hotels
- Maximum $250/night in major metropolitan areas
- Maximum $175/night in other locations
- Incidental charges up to $25/day (tips, phone calls)

### Meals

#### While Traveling
- Breakfast: Up to $20
- Lunch: Up to $30
- Dinner: Up to $50
- Alcohol: Up to $20 per meal (when with clients or business partners)

#### Team Meals
- Team lunches: Up to $30 per person
- Team dinners: Up to $60 per person
- Holiday parties: Pre-approval required

### Equipment and Supplies

#### Approved Equipment
- Computer accessories (keyboard, mouse, monitor stands)
- Office supplies
- Software licenses (with IT approval)

#### Home Office
- Desk: Up to $500
- Chair: Up to $400
- Other furniture: Up to $200
- Internet upgrade: Up to $100 one-time

### Professional Development
- Conference registration: Pre-approval required
- Books and subscriptions: Up to $200/year
- Online courses: Up to $500/year with manager approval

## Non-Reimbursable Expenses

The following are NOT eligible for reimbursement:
- Personal expenses (entertainment, souvenirs, personal phone calls)
- Traffic violations or parking tickets
- Personal travel insurance
- First-class upgrades without pre-approval
- Expenses for family members or guests
- Gym memberships (see separate benefits policy)
- Tobacco products or excessive alcohol
- Lost or stolen items

## Receipt Requirements

All expense reports must include:
1. Itemized receipt (not just credit card slips)
2. Business purpose explanation
3. Names of attendees for client meals
4. Date and location
5. Vendor name

Missing receipts require a lost receipt affidavit for amounts over $25.

## Processing and Payment

- Expense reports are processed every two weeks
- Reimbursement is paid on the next available payroll
- Direct deposit is required for reimbursement
- Questions about reimbursement status: expenses@company.com

## Compliance

- All expenses must have a legitimate business purpose
- Falsifying expense reports is a terminable offense
- Receipts may be audited up to 12 months after submission
- Repeated policy violations may result in reduced reimbursement privileges

## Exceptions

Requests for exceptions to this policy must be:
1. Submitted in writing to HR
2. Approved by a department director
3. Documented in the expense system

## Contact

For questions about expense reimbursement, contact the Finance team at expenses@company.com
