# Benefits Overview

## Health Insurance

### Medical Coverage
- **PPO Plan**: Lower deductible, broader network
  - Employee only: $150/month
  - Employee + spouse: $350/month
  - Employee + children: $300/month
  - Family: $500/month

- **HDHP Plan**: Lower premiums, higher deductible, HSA eligible
  - Employee only: $75/month
  - Employee + spouse: $175/month
  - Employee + children: $150/month
  - Family: $250/month

### Dental Coverage
- Preventive care: 100% covered
- Basic procedures: 80% covered
- Major procedures: 50% covered
- Annual maximum: $2,000
- Orthodontia: 50% covered (up to $1,500 lifetime)

### Vision Coverage
- Annual eye exam: $10 copay
- Frames: $150 allowance every 24 months
- Lenses: $25 copay
- Contacts: $150 allowance every 12 months

## Health Savings Account (HSA)

For HDHP participants:
- Company contribution: $500/year (individual), $1,000/year (family)
- Employee contribution limits: $4,150 (individual), $8,300 (family)
- Funds roll over indefinitely
- Investment options available at $1,000 balance

## Flexible Spending Account (FSA)

### Healthcare FSA
- Contribution limit: $3,050/year
- $610 carryover maximum
- Use for medical, dental, vision expenses

### Dependent Care FSA
- Contribution limit: $5,000/year
- Use for childcare, elder care expenses
- Pre-tax benefit

## Retirement Benefits

### 401(k) Plan
- Employee contribution: up to 90% of salary (IRS limit)
- Company match: 100% of first 3%, 50% of next 2%
- Immediate vesting of employee contributions
- Company match vests over 3 years (33% per year)

### Roth 401(k)
- After-tax contributions
- Tax-free withdrawals in retirement
- Subject to Roth income limits

## Life Insurance

- Basic life insurance: 1x annual salary (company-paid)
- Supplemental life insurance: up to 5x salary (employee-paid)
- Evidence of insurability required above 3x salary

## Disability Insurance

### Short-Term Disability (STD)
- Duration: Up to 12 weeks
- Benefit: 60% of base salary
- Waiting period: 7 days
- Company-paid for full-time employees

### Long-Term Disability (LTD)
- Duration: Until age 65 or Social Security retirement age
- Benefit: 60% of base salary (maximum $10,000/month)
- Waiting period: 90 days
- Company-paid for full-time employees

## Additional Benefits

### Employee Assistance Program (EAP)
- Free, confidential counseling
- 8 sessions per year
- Covers mental health, legal, financial issues

### Gym Membership
- Reimbursement up to $50/month
- Must use approved gym network

### Tuition Reimbursement
- Up to $5,250/year for approved programs
- Must be employed 1+ year
- Must remain employed 1 year after completion

### Commuter Benefits
- Pre-tax transit: Up to $315/month
- Pre-tax parking: Up to $315/month

### Parental Leave

#### Birth Parents
- 16 weeks paid leave
- Additional 4 weeks for complications

#### Non-Birth Parents
- 8 weeks paid leave

#### Adoption
- 12 weeks paid leave
- $5,000 adoption assistance

## Eligibility

- Full-time employees: Eligible first of month following start date
- Part-time employees: Pro-rated benefits based on hours
- Seasonal employees: Limited benefits

## Enrollment

- Open enrollment: November 1-30 each year
- New hire enrollment: Within 30 days of start date
- Qualifying life events: 30 days to make changes
