# Workplace Conduct Policy

## Purpose

This policy establishes standards for professional conduct, ensuring a respectful, safe, and productive work environment for all employees.

## Core Values

- **Respect**: Treat all colleagues with dignity and consideration
- **Integrity**: Act honestly and ethically in all situations
- **Excellence**: Strive for quality in all work
- **Collaboration**: Work together to achieve shared goals
- **Inclusion**: Value diverse perspectives and backgrounds

## Professional Standards

### Attendance and Punctuality
- Arrive on time for work and meetings
- Notify manager of absences in advance when possible
- Follow call-in procedures for sick leave
- Use PTO for planned absences

### Workplace Appearance
- Dress appropriately for your role and work setting
- Business casual for office work
- Professional attire for client meetings
- Follow department-specific guidelines

### Communication
- Use professional language and tone
- Respond to communications promptly
- Be clear and concise in written communications
- Practice active listening

### Use of Company Resources
- Use company equipment for business purposes
- Limit personal use of company resources
- Protect company assets and information
- Report waste or abuse

## Harassment and Discrimination

### Zero Tolerance Policy

The company maintains a zero-tolerance policy for:
- Harassment of any kind
- Discrimination based on protected characteristics
- Retaliation against those who report violations

### Protected Characteristics
- Race, color, religion
- Sex, gender identity, sexual orientation
- Age
- Disability
- National origin
- Genetic information
- Military status
- Any other protected characteristic under law

### Prohibited Conduct

**Verbal Harassment**:
- Slurs, epithets, or name-calling
- Offensive jokes or comments
- Intimidation or threats
- Ridicule or mockery

**Physical Harassment**:
- Unwanted physical contact
- Physical intimidation
- Assault
- Invasion of personal space

**Visual Harassment**:
- Offensive images or materials
- Offensive gestures
- Staring or following

**Sexual Harassment**:
- Unwelcome sexual advances
- Requesting sexual favors
- Other verbal or physical conduct of a sexual nature

### Reporting Harassment

**How to Report**:
1. Report to your manager (if appropriate)
2. Contact HR directly
3. Use anonymous hotline: 1-800-XXX-XXXX
4. Email: ethics@company.com

**What to Include**:
- Names of people involved
- Date, time, location
- Description of incident(s)
- Names of witnesses
- Any supporting documentation

### Investigation Process

1. Report received and acknowledged
2. Initial assessment conducted
3. Investigation opened if warranted
4. Parties interviewed
5. Findings documented
6. Appropriate action taken
7. Follow-up with reporter

### Non-Retaliation

Retaliation against anyone who:
- Reports harassment
- Participates in investigation
- Opposes discriminatory practices

is strictly prohibited and will result in disciplinary action.

## Workplace Violence Prevention

### Prohibited Behaviors
- Threatening or violent behavior
- Possession of weapons on company property
- Stalking
- Verbal intimidation
- Property damage

### Prevention Measures
- Background checks for all employees
- Security measures at all locations
- Employee assistance program
- Clear reporting procedures

### Reporting Violence

Immediately report:
- Direct threats
- Physical violence
- Stalking behavior
- Concerning statements about harm

Contact Security: security@company.com
Emergency: 911

## Ethics and Integrity

### Conflicts of Interest

Disclose and manage situations involving:
- Financial interests in vendors or competitors
- Outside employment
- Family members in similar industries
- Serving on outside boards

### Accepting Gifts

- Gifts under $50: Generally acceptable
- Gifts $50-$100: Requires manager approval
- Gifts over $100: Must be declined or approved by HR
- Never solicit gifts

### Confidential Information

- Protect company confidential information
- Do not share proprietary information
- Follow data security policies
- Respect competitor confidentiality

### Honest Representation

- Accurately represent your qualifications
- Do not falsify records or reports
- Report errors promptly
- Maintain accurate time records

## Disciplinary Process

### Progressive Discipline

Typically follows these steps:
1. Verbal warning
2. Written warning
3. Final written warning
4. Termination

### Considerations

Disciplinary actions consider:
- Severity of violation
- Employee's history
- Impact on others
- Mitigating circumstances
- Consistency with past actions

### Immediate Termination

May result from:
- Violence or threats of violence
- Theft or fraud
- Gross misconduct
- Serious policy violations
- Illegal activity

## Performance Issues

Performance concerns are addressed separately from conduct:
- Regular performance reviews
- Performance improvement plans
- Additional training or support
- Transfer or demotion options

## Policy Administration

### Questions
Contact HR for questions about this policy.

### Training
All employees must complete:
- Annual code of conduct training
- Harassment prevention training
- Ethics awareness training

### Policy Updates
This policy is reviewed annually and updated as needed.

## At-Will Employment

This policy does not create an employment contract. Employment remains at-will unless modified by law.

## Acknowledgment

All employees must acknowledge reading and understanding this policy upon hire and annually thereafter.
