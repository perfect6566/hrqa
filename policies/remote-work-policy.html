Remote Work Policy

1. Overview

Our company supports flexible work arrangements to promote work-life balance, reduce commuting stress, and maintain productivity. This policy outlines the guidelines and requirements for remote work arrangements.

2. Eligibility

2.1 General Requirements

  • Completed at least 90 days of employment
  • Current performance rating of "Meets Expectations" or higher
  • No active disciplinary actions in the past 6 months
  • Position suitable for remote work (as determined by manager)

2.2 Work Arrangement Types

TypeDescriptionEligibility
Fully RemoteWork from home full-timeSpecific roles only, director approval required
HybridSplit between office and homeAll eligible employees, manager approval
Occasional Remote1-2 days per week from homeAll eligible employees

3. Equipment and Setup

3.1 Company-Provided Equipment

  • Laptop computer
  • Monitor (for hybrid/remote employees)
  • Keyboard and mouse
  • Headset for video calls

3.2 Employee Responsibilities

  • Maintain a dedicated, distraction-free workspace
  • Ensure reliable high-speed internet connection (minimum 25 Mbps)
  • Keep equipment secure and in good condition
  • Report any equipment issues within 24 hours

4. Security Requirements

Important: All remote work must comply with our Data Security Policy. Failure to follow security protocols may result in revocation of remote work privileges.
  • Use VPN for all work activities
  • Enable full-disk encryption on all devices
  • Use company-approved password manager
  • Enable two-factor authentication on all accounts
  • Lock screen when away from workspace
  • Do not use public WiFi without VPN

5. Work Hours and Availability

5.1 Core Hours

All employees must be available during core hours: 10:00 AM - 3:00 PM local time, regardless of work location.

5.2 Response Times

  • Email/Slack: Within 2 hours during core hours
  • Urgent matters: Within 30 minutes
  • Calendar invites: Respond within 24 hours

6. Working from Different Locations

Note: Working from locations other than your registered home office requires prior approval and has specific requirements detailed below.

6.1 Out-of-State Remote Work

Employees may work from a different state for up to 30 consecutive days per calendar year with manager approval.

  • Submit request at least 5 business days in advance
  • Ensure ability to attend all required meetings
  • Confirm tax and employment law implications with HR
  • Maintain primary work location at registered address

6.2 International Remote Work

Working outside the country requires executive approval and is subject to:

  • Tax compliance requirements
  • Visa and work permit regulations
  • Minimum 30-day advance notice
  • Maximum 90 days per calendar year

7. Requests and Approvals

7.1 Standard Request Process

  1. Discuss with direct manager
  2. Submit remote work request form in HR portal
  3. Complete required training modules
  4. HR reviews for compliance
  5. Final approval from department head

8. Performance and Management

8.1 Performance Expectations

  • Same performance standards apply regardless of work location
  • Regular one-on-one meetings with manager (weekly)
  • Participation in all required team meetings
  • Completion of deliverables on schedule

8.2 Communication Requirements

  • Update calendar with availability daily
  • Set status in Slack/Teams appropriately
  • Participate in video calls with camera on during meetings
  • Maintain regular communication with team members

10. Policy Violations

Violations of this policy may result in:

  • Verbal warning
  • Written warning
  • Revocation of remote work privileges
  • Disciplinary action up to and including termination

11. Review and Amendments

This policy is reviewed annually and may be amended at any time with reasonable notice. Employees will be notified of significant changes via email and HR portal announcement.
