# Leave of Absence Policy

## Overview

This policy covers leaves of absence beyond standard PTO, including medical leave, family leave, military leave, and other qualifying absences.

## Types of Leave

### Medical Leave
For employee illness or injury:

**Eligibility**: All employees
**Duration**: Up to 12 weeks per 12-month period (FMLA)
**Pay**: Uses accumulated PTO, then unpaid

**Process**:
1. Notify manager as soon as reasonably possible
2. Submit medical certification
3. HR reviews and approves
4. Maintain benefits during leave

### Family and Medical Leave (FMLA)

**Eligibility Requirements**:
- 12+ months of employment
- 1,250+ hours worked in past 12 months
- Work at location with 50+ employees within 75 miles

**Qualifying Reasons**:
- Birth and care of newborn child
- Adoption or foster care placement
- Care for spouse, child, or parent with serious health condition
- Employee's own serious health condition

**Benefits During FMLA**:
- Health insurance maintained
- Position restored upon return
- Seniority preserved

### Parental Leave

**Birth Parents**:
- 16 weeks paid leave
- Can be taken within 12 months of birth

**Non-Birth Parents**:
- 8 weeks paid leave
- Can be taken within 12 months of birth/adoption

**Adoption Leave**:
- 12 weeks paid leave
- Additional $5,000 reimbursement for adoption expenses

### Military Leave

**Active Duty**:
- Up to 5 years for cumulative leave
- Position restored upon return
- Seniority and benefits preserved

**Reserve Training**:
- 2 weeks per year
- Paid leave for annual training

**Notification Required**:
- 30 days advance notice when possible
- Written notice for expected leave
- Copy of orders

### Jury Duty

**Policy**:
- Full pay during jury service
- Up to 20 days per year
- Provide jury summons to HR

### Voting Leave

**Policy**:
- Up to 2 hours paid leave to vote
- Schedule with manager
- Available for federal, state, local elections

### Bereavement Leave

**Immediate Family** (spouse, child, parent, sibling):
- Up to 5 days paid leave

**Extended Family** (grandparent, in-law, aunt/uncle):
- Up to 3 days paid leave

**Other**:
- 1 day paid leave for attendance at funeral

**Additional Time**:
- May use PTO for extended bereavement
- Unpaid leave available for exceptional circumstances

### Personal Leave

**Eligibility**: Employees with 1+ year tenure
**Duration**: Up to 4 weeks
**Pay**: Unpaid
**Approval**: Director-level approval required

**Purpose**:
- Extended personal matters
- Sabbatical-style breaks
- Non-medical reasons

## Request Process

### Standard Requests
1. Submit written request to manager
2. Manager recommends approval/denial
3. HR reviews
4. Employee notified of decision

### Emergency Requests
1. Notify manager as soon as possible
2. Submit request retroactively
3. Provide documentation when available

## Maintaining Benefits

During leave:
- Health insurance continues (employee portion may be required)
- PTO accrual pauses after 30 days of unpaid leave
- Seniority maintained
- Performance review delayed

## Returning from Leave

### Requirements
- Provide fitness-for-duty certification (medical leave)
- Return on agreed-upon date
- Contact HR if circumstances change

### Reinstatement
- Same position or equivalent
- No reduction in pay or benefits
- Updated training may be required

## Intermittent Leave

For ongoing conditions:
- May take leave in separate blocks
- Minimum increment: 1 hour
- Requires medical certification
- Subject to operational needs
