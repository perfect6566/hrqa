# Employee Onboarding Policy

## Purpose

This policy outlines the onboarding process for new employees to ensure a smooth transition and successful start with the company.

## Pre-Start Preparation

### 1-2 Weeks Before Start Date
- HR sends welcome email with start date, time, location details
- IT prepares equipment and access credentials
- Manager prepares workspace and schedule
- Onboarding buddy assigned (for new graduate hires)

### Required Documents
All new hires must provide:
- Completed I-9 form with supporting documentation
- W-4 tax withholding form
- Direct deposit authorization
- Emergency contact information
- Completed background check authorization
- Signed offer letter and NDA

## First Day

### Arrival
- Start time: 9:00 AM
- Check in at reception
- Badge and access credentials issued
- Workspace tour

### Morning Session (9:00 AM - 12:00 PM)
- Welcome from HR
- Benefits enrollment session
- IT setup and security training
- Overview of company policies

### Afternoon Session (1:00 PM - 4:00 PM)
- Department orientation with manager
- Meet team members
- Review job responsibilities and initial projects
- Set up 30-day goals

### First Week Goals
- Complete all required compliance training
- Set up all work tools and systems
- Schedule 1:1 meetings with key stakeholders
- Begin first assigned project or task

## Required Training

All new employees must complete within 30 days:

### Week 1
- [ ] Security Awareness Training
- [ ] Code of Conduct Review
- [ ] Harassment Prevention Training
- [ ] Benefits Enrollment

### Week 2
- [ ] Role-Specific Training (as assigned)
- [ ] Department Processes Overview
- [ ] Communication Tools Training (email, Slack, Teams)

### Week 3-4
- [ ] Complete all assigned compliance training
- [ ] 30-Day Check-in with Manager

## 30-Day Review

At 30 days, new employees meet with their manager to:
1. Review initial goals and progress
2. Address any questions or concerns
3. Provide feedback on onboarding experience
4. Adjust goals for the next 60 days

## 60-Day Review

At 60 days, employees receive informal feedback:
- Performance check-in
- Clarify expectations
- Identify any training needs
- Confirm role fit

## 90-Day Review

At 90 days (end of probationary period):
- Formal performance review
- Transition to regular employee status
- Discuss career development goals
- Set annual performance objectives

## Onboarding for Remote Employees

Remote employees follow the same timeline with adjustments:

### Pre-Start
- Equipment shipped to home address
- Virtual workspace setup guide provided
- Video conferencing tested

### First Day (Virtual)
- Same schedule, conducted via video call
- Virtual team introductions
- Shared document walkthrough

### First Week
- Daily check-ins with manager
- Virtual team events
- Asynchronous onboarding activities

### First Month
- In-person visit if feasible (travel provided)
- Quarterly in-person team meetings required

## Manager Responsibilities

Managers are responsible for:
1. Preparing workspace and materials before employee start date
2. Scheduling department orientation
3. Introducing employee to key contacts
4. Setting clear 30/60/90 day expectations
5. Conducting required review meetings
6. Providing regular feedback and support

## New Employee Responsibilities

New employees should:
1. Complete all required paperwork before or on first day
2. Arrive on time and prepared to learn
3. Ask questions when unsure
4. Seek feedback proactively
5. Complete all training on schedule
6. Provide onboarding feedback

## Onboarding Checklist

- [ ] Send welcome email

- [ ] Process paperwork

### Manager Tasks

- [ ] Schedule meetings
- [ ] Create 30-day plan

### IT Tasks
